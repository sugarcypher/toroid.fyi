Part 1 — Macro and meta analysis

1.1 One loop, described four times, at three resolutions (verified structure; synthesis of the equivalence)

The site carries four descriptions of a closed extraction cycle, each at a different station count:

Instrument Stations Order
Polymorphic Matrix (2026-03) 4 apparatus → convergence (MIF) → erosion → feedback
Substrate Attack (2026-09) 3 cut the layer → frame the cut → collect on the damage
Removal Economy (2026-05) 6 substrate → threat → removal → harm → extraction → cost of lives
Deception Arcane chains (v0.3+) 3 S3 block the fix → sustained crisis → S6 capture

These are the same operator sampled at different rates. The common six-phase form, into which all four map without remainder:

  1. Substrate — the load-bearing layer in place (Matrix: the polity the apparatus acts on; Removal I)
  2. Framing — the layer recoded as threat, waste, or incompetence (Removal II; Substrate "frame the cut"; Arcane S5)
  3. Cut — the operation (Matrix apparatus; Removal III; Substrate "cut"; Arcane S3 refusal of the fix; every 7.x cell)
  4. Harm — the material residue (Matrix erosion; Removal IV; the register's baselines)
  5. Capture — value re-booked privately (Removal V; Substrate "collect"; Arcane S1/S6)
  6. Reinvestment — harm converted into the justification and the appropriation for the next cut (Matrix feedback; Removal "the harm of each turn financing the next"; the ratchet doctrines)

The phase the site under-measures is 6. Every instrument asserts that harm re-enters as justification; none measures the conversion (harm → appropriation → next cut) as a quantity. The Removal page states it ("$170B booked as a loss … routed into Station V") but it is the only place.

1.2 The second periodicity the torus promised and the instruments never used (synthesis)

Every page says the torus is "doubly periodic". Only the Matrix uses two circles (major: the five-vector pentagon; minor: apparatus → MIF → erosion → feedback). The Catalysis Toroid nests two tori instead; the Removal loop is one circle of six; the opening ring is one circle of seven. The minor circle has been decoration since March.

The site's own method supplies the missing cycle. Read across pages, the work follows a closed loop of its own:

observe → name → measure → predict → check → correct → observe

  • observe: the Catalysis Toroid cells, the register's evidence annex
  • name: the predicates, the Matrix (the first naming), the Substrate Attack page, the book
  • measure: the Deception Arcane signatures and composites
  • predict: the register's locked predictions, Sches as the commitment model
  • check: dated checkpoints, negative controls, adversarial tests T1/T2, the pool-footage baseline
  • correct: _revision_note, withdrawn claims (the 70/85 count justification), the ρ −0.89 → −0.57 de-bias

That is a second independent cycle. Two independent cycles is exactly the condition under which a torus is the right 3-manifold boundary and not an ornament: the surface is the product of the two circles, and every point on it is a pairing (phase of the extraction cycle, phase of the measurement cycle).

The payoff is that obfuscation gets a geometric definition. The Substrate Attack page already says the measuring apparatus (courts, inspectors general, audits, press) is itself load-bearing and is being cut. On the two-cycle surface that is literal: the regime's cycle runs around the major circle; the observer's cycle runs around the minor; obfuscation is the regime's work to keep the minor circle from closing — IG firings cut observe, manufactured incoherence (S5) cuts name, anti-remediation (S3) cuts correct, the press ban cuts observe and check at once. The register's PRESS-ACCESS prediction is therefore the first instrument on the site pointed at the minor circle itself: measurement of the attack on measurement. That reflexive position (observer inside the observed system, second-order cybernetics — von Foerster's term, offered as a link not a citation) is the site's actual meta-claim and nothing currently draws it.

1.3 Each instrument is a different calculus operation on the same field (synthesis)

Instrument Operation
Catalysis Toroid the state — who acts on whom, position on the surface
Polymorphic Matrix the flow field — direction of circulation
Deception Arcane composite the level — a scalar reading of a state
Arcane temporal layer the first derivative (velocity), the count-derivative (breadth), the timing correlation (synchrony)
Register the forward integral — a prediction is a stated future value with a check date
Removal Economy one trajectory — a single particle traced around the loop
Sches the generator — how agents committing blind produce the loop at all
Substrate Attack the invariant — the conserved quantity being moved
Named and Counted the units — what gets counted and in what

Nothing on the site states this division of labour. Visitors are told the instruments "share a topology"; they are not told that they are complementary operations and that the set is nearly complete. "Nearly": there is no second-derivative (acceleration of capture) and no instrument for phase 6.

1.4 The conserved quantity, and a cross-disciplinary reading (synthesis, flagged as analogy)

The Substrate Attack page names the single currency: the financial health of the nation. The Removal page quantifies one turn ($96.7B paid in; $170B appropriated; $8.9B/yr destroyed per million removed; 31 deaths off-ledger). Put together, the site is describing a transfer, not a destruction, with a loss term: value moves from the commons to private capture; what cannot be transferred (lives, separations) is the loss term the contracts keep off the books.

Thermodynamic analogy, offered as a thinking tool, not a claim: a heat engine moves energy across a gradient and does work; the insulation is what stops the back-reaction. Here the gradient is commons → capture; the work is extraction; the insulation is obfuscation; the waste heat is the unpriced harm. The Arcane's five signatures then read as engine parameters — S1 the gradient (stated vs realized beneficiary), S2 selective conductivity, S3 a closed relief valve, S4 the material the engine is built from, S5 the insulation. That S5 carries the highest weight (0.30) is consistent with the analogy: without insulation the engine stalls. This is why the analogy is worth having on the page as a reading aid and nowhere as a finding.

1.7 Scale ladder (synthesis)

cell (one actor on one target) → architecture (a program, scored) → regime (the mode mix, the vortices) → condition (the substrate attack) → and back: the condition selects which cells are cheap to open next. That return edge is phase 6 again. The ladder is the minor circle at a different grain, which is the reason the two-cycle model, and not a three-cycle one, is enough: the scale ladder and the method cycle move together.

Part 2 — Audit: missing connections, false connections, errors, improvements

Severity: A false or broken in what the visitor sees · B inconsistency in stated facts · C design / UX · D upgrade.

A — false or broken

A1 Dual-role ids break actor labels (verified, 12 nodes). Thirteen ids exist as both an actor and a target (doj, state_dept, congress_maj, iran_post, foundation_models, big_tech, cartels, ftc, fcc, hhs, civil_society, public_sector_workers, scotus). nodeLabels is keyed by bare id, so the target's label overwrites the actor's; the actor spheres for Big Tech, Congress, DOJ, FTC, FCC, HHS, foundation models, civil society and the public-sector workforce can never show a label (permanently display:none from init). Fix: key labels by role (a:/t:). The adjacency merge of dual-role ids is correct behaviour (one entity, two roles) and is kept.

A2 Relationship layers are 39% invisible (verified). 30 actors have no 7.x cell and are filtered out of the rendered set; every R/B/P edge touching them is silently skipped. Not drawn: 51 of 130 R-cells, 9 of 50 B-wormholes, 4 of 17 P-cells. Among the missing: R038 (Executive → Federalist Society staffing), which the Deception Arcane glossary cites by id as its S4 anchor. The evidence layer cannot show the cell the measurement layer points to. Fix: render any actor that has at least one edge of any layer; show the honest count where the visitor can see it.

A3 Vortex source that does not exist (verified). capital vortex lists sovereign_wealth; no such actor. The sovereign-wealth actors are saudi, uae, qatar (State Allies). Fix: point at the actors that exist (saudi, uae; qatar has no cells and would still not draw until A2).

A4 scotus missing from actorFlow (verified). Only actor without a hemisphere assignment; falls through to whatever the placement code defaults to. Fix: assign (upper — direction-source by analogy with the judiciary cluster's role in the cells).

C — design and UX

C1 Opening ring order is roughly chronological (poly, sabbot, removal, book, substrate, sches, matrix) and carries no meaning. With the two-cycle model the ring can be ordered as the measurement cycle (observe → name → measure → predict → check → correct), so the first thing a visitor sees is the method, and the new gate sits where it belongs.

C2 Opening ring has eight gates after the addition; label width 210px at radius 2.5 is tight at the 4 and 8 o'clock positions on 1280-wide screens. Radius 2.65 and camera z 8.2 keep the labels clear.

C3 The ring is a set of toroids with no connective tissue. A faint closed line through the gate centres makes the ring read as a cycle rather than a menu, at near-zero visual cost.

C4 Site-wide: the Removal page footer promises "integration with the Catalysis Toroid cell references (B040, 7.277, 7.328)" as future work. The cells exist. The toroid has no deep-link parameter, so the promise cannot be kept without one.

C5 New page must carry the same no-JS static index and reduced-motion path as /index.html.
