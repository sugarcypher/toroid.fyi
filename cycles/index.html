The Two Cycles

Every page on this site says the torus is doubly periodic. Until now only one period was used. The regime runs the first cycle — the extraction loop every instrument here describes. The observer runs the second — observe, name, measure, predict, check, correct. Two independent cycles make a torus; obfuscation is the work of keeping the second one from closing.

One loop, described four times

The Matrix (four stages), the Substrate Attack (three beats), the Removal Economy (six stations) and the Deception Arcane's operation chains (three links) are one closed operator sampled at different rates. The six-phase form below is the common refinement; every existing description maps onto it without remainder. The Removal Economy's sixth station — the cost of lives, off the ledger — is not a phase: it is the loss term, drawn here as what falls off the surface into the hole.

What each instrument does to the same field

The instruments are not eight views of one thing; they are eight different operations on one field, and the set is nearly complete. Stating the division of labour is what makes the gaps legible.

Missing operations: a second derivative (is capture accelerating?) and any measure of phase 6 — the conversion of harm into the next appropriation — which every instrument asserts and none quantifies. The Removal Economy's per-turn numbers ($170B appropriated against $8.9B/yr of revenue destroyed per million removed) are the one worked instance.

Where the regime cuts the second cycle

The Substrate Attack page says the means of seeing are load-bearing and are being cut. On this surface that is literal: each documented move lands on a phase of the measurement cycle. The register's press-access prediction is the first instrument on the site aimed at the second cycle itself — measurement of the attack on measurement.

    Reading aid · the engine

    Offered as an analogy for thinking, not as a finding. A heat engine moves energy across a gradient and does work; insulation stops the back-reaction. Here the gradient is commons → capture, the work is extraction, the insulation is obfuscation, the waste heat is the unpriced harm. The Deception Arcane's five signatures read as engine parameters: S1 the gradient (stated vs realized beneficiary), S2 selective conductivity, S3 a closed relief valve, S4 the material the engine is built from, S5 the insulation. S5 carrying the highest weight is consistent with the analogy — without insulation the engine stalls — and that consistency is the whole of the analogy's evidential value.

    What would falsify the two-cycle reading

    It loses if the instruments' coverage does not partition along two independent axes — if, say, every instrument that observes also measures and predicts, so the second circle collapses to a point; if the regime's documented moves against oversight land at random rather than on identifiable phases of the measurement cycle; or if phases 1–6 cannot be shown to recur in the same order across the Matrix, the Removal loop and the Arcane chains. The first two are checkable against the coverage grid above; the third against the equivalence table.
