## Instrument capabilities (v0.33)

**Geometry**: Nested tire-and-inner-tube toroidal architecture. Outer torus (magenta wireframe) hosts actor spheres; inner torus (cyan wireframe) hosts target octahedra; central yellow icosahedron is the Intent-Action Catalyst that operations route through. Silver equatorial rings mark the transformation boundary where origination becomes consequence.

**Edge types**: Four parallel edge layers documenting different analytical primitives. 7.x cells (actor→target operations) with six intent channels — STRUCT (substrate, routes via exterior bow-point), CONV (emergent convergence), COORD (orchestrated coordination), OPP (opportunistic alignment), INACT (deliberate inaction with downstream proofs), UNK (operational role unclear). R-cells (actor↔actor coordination) across six relation types: ALLY/OWN/THRU/STAFF/FUND/INST. B-cells (actor↔actor benefit-inferred wormholes) rendered as ghost-white aurora tubes outside the operational geometry. P-cells (actor↔actor polarity tensions) rendered as alternating gold/violet sine-wave lines documenting opposing pressures within the operating coalition.

**Filter system**: Channel filter (6 channels), evidence tier filter (D/I/S — Documented/Inferable/Speculative), operational mode filter (42-mode Multimalocracy taxonomy), degree-of-separation filter (1°–5° BFS traversal from selected node).

**Interaction**: Every actor and target is clickable. Selection isolates a node's network at the current degree-of-separation depth. Cluster and target-group selection isolate the corresponding membership. Live degree counter shows cumulative node-count at each depth, exposing the network's multiplicative structure.

**View modes**: OUTER (actor-only view), INNER (target-only view), GLOBAL (reveals 17-country International Sovereignty target group), THEME (dark/light mode), VORTEX (six consolidation attractors with multi-source capture pipelines: Power, Capital, Money, Attention, Intelligence, Narrative).

## Operating philosophy

The instrument is built around three methodological commitments:

**Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy is the user's originating insight** — rule by sabotage (the sabbotarchic regime form) operating through deliberate obfuscation that frames the sabotage as incompetence (the obfuscratic operational signature). The concept was originally coined as *Sabbotarcracy* and formally renamed in 2026 to the bipartite *Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy* to expose the regime-form-plus-operational-signature structure the single-word neologism collapsed. The Multimalocracy 42-mode taxonomy is a discriminative-vocabulary apparatus built around that originating insight. The INACT channel makes the obfuscratic operation legible in the instrument: each INACT cell pairs an inaction with its documented downstream consequence.

**Coalitions are not internally uniform**. P-cells document polarity tensions within the operating coalition. The Reactionary pole and the Accelerationist pole operate in convergent operational space but with opposing theoretical priors. Treating coalitions as monolithic misses the tension architecture that the schema makes legible.

**Operations sustain through substrate**. B-cells (wormholes) document benefit-inferred relationships that operate without formal coordination. The substrate ecology of mutual-benefit relationships is what sustains operations across administrative transitions; the substrate is the architecture that matters more than any specific operational episode.
