The composite and its bands

The five signatures fold into one number per architecture. Each is scored zero to one hundred; the composite is the weighted average.

Index = 0.25·S1 + 0.20·S2 + 0.15·S3 + 0.10·S4 + 0.30·S5

The index sorts into four bands.

BandRangeReading
Genuine0–30Consistent with honest competence or honest failure; asymmetry low.
Mixed30–70Some signatures present, not concentrated; more analysis needed.
High-Asymmetry70–85The directional signature is satisfied.
Extreme-Asymmetry85–100The asymmetry is concentrated across all five dimensions.

The thresholds are not pulled from air. Reaching 70 takes the high range on at least four of the five dimensions; reaching 85 takes it on all five at once. They will move as more architectures are scored, and any move gets logged, not slipped in.

On the precision I will not fake. These scores are not measurements drawn from a sampling distribution. They are one analyst’s judgments, and a confidence interval computed over a sample of one is fabricated precision. So no score in this book carries a ±. Read every number here as a claim about which band, never as a precise quantity.

The evidence tags

  • Occurred. A documented event, verifiable against the contemporaneous record: a statute, a court filing, an agency notice, dated reporting. Eligible to anchor a claim about a realized outcome.
  • Projected. A modeled or forecast outcome, a CBO estimate, a mortality model. Legitimate evidence, but it may anchor only a projected claim, and it must say so in the row it sits in.
  • Contested. Magnitude, attribution, or characterization is disputed. The tag does not, by itself, lower confidence. Whether it does depends on the quality of the contestation.
  • Correlation. A relationship has been measured between variables, with no claim about causal direction.

The two tests it has not yet faced

Part VIII states both in advance, with their predicted outcomes on the record, before any scoring. Convict a friend: point the five signatures, S5 above all, at an architecture I am politically sympathetic to. Acquit an enemy: find one action of the administration I oppose that, measured honestly, is ordinary symmetric incompetence, and score it Genuine or Mixed. Neither has been run.

Until the book contradicts its author somewhere you can see it, you are right to suspect it has only ever agreed with them, and a measurement that only ever agrees with its maker has measured, among other things, its maker.
