IWhy a torus

The systems we live inside don't have edges.

Most strategic analysis is done on grids. A grid has edges; an edge has an outside. Outside the grid is where the analyst stands. The analyst assumes a vantage that the system itself never occupies.

This works for chess. It does not work for the systems that actually shape upstream outcomes — political pressure architectures, multi-agent commitment regimes, perception apparatuses, capital and attention circulation, regulatory captures. In those systems, pressure does not exit. It loops back. It re-enters the apparatus that produced it. Flanking is not a maneuver; it is a permanent topological condition. The actors who appear to be at the periphery are simultaneously at the center, because there is no periphery.

A torus is the simplest surface that captures this structurally: bounded (you cannot leave it), edgeless (there is no outside to step into), doubly periodic (motion in either direction returns to origin). Treating the substrate as toroidal is not aesthetic. It is an instrument-design commitment to letting the geometry do work the analyst would otherwise have to do by hand — and would, almost always, get wrong.

The decision to use a torus is the decision to stop pretending the system has an outside the analyst can stand on.

Each artifact on this site uses toroidal substrate as a research instrument, not as decoration. The three instruments study three different layers: relational architecture of operating coalitions; signature pattern that distinguishes engineered incompetence from genuine incompetence; and the irrevocable-commitment dynamic that connects them. A fourth, the precursor, captures the moment the topology emerged.

IVThe two-instrument stack

Documenting and measuring are different epistemic operations.

The Catalysis Toroid renders the WHO and HOW — relational architecture, intent channels, evidence tiers (Documented / Inferable / Speculative). The Sabbotarchy framework scores the WHAT IT IS — regime character measured on five empirical signature dimensions. Each instrument is independently meaningful; together they support an iterative analytical workflow that neither could support alone.

VThe signature instrument

Engineered incompetence has a signature. So does the genuine kind. They are different.

The Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy is a measurement apparatus, not an accusation. It scores government-services-redirection architectures on five empirically-grounded dimensions, each constructed so the signature of engineered incompetence is structurally distinguishable from the signature of genuine incompetence. An architecture scoring below 30 is consistent with genuine incompetence or genuine competence and the framework provides no basis for further diagnostic claim.

S1w = 0.25

Outcome-distribution asymmetry

Divergence between the stated policy beneficiary and the realized beneficiary. Genuine policy produces symmetric distributions; engineered incompetence produces directional concentration toward extraction architectures.

S2w = 0.20

Domain-selective competence

Differential between public-facing function delivery and extractive function delivery within the same operational architecture. Surgical precision in operations against, buffoonery in operations for, with the same personnel.

S3w = 0.15

Anti-remediation response

Slope of resistance-intensity as a function of remediation cost. Genuine incompetence: positive slope (expensive fixes resisted more — budget logic). Obfuscratic sabbotarchy: flat-high or inverted slope (free fixes resisted as strongly as expensive ones — structural protection).

S4w = 0.10

Recruitment selection

Three-axis pattern: technical-competence retention × loyalty-signal advancement × dissent-tolerance ratio. Engineered architectures concurrently produce low technical retention, high loyalty advancement, and near-zero dissent tolerance.

S5w = 0.30

Information-architecture asymmetry

Ratio of internal information coherence (within the regime) to external information coherence (the public information ecosystem the regime produces). The cleanest operational definition. Weighted highest because the asymmetry signature has the fewest alternative explanations.

Composite: Index = 0.25·S1 + 0.20·S2 + 0.15·S3 + 0.10·S4 + 0.30·S5. Four bands: 0–30 Genuine pattern, 30–70 Mixed signature, 70–85 Engineered pattern, 85–100 Extreme signature.

VIThe vocabulary apparatus

Three predicates the standard vocabulary collapses.

Naming is analytical work. The framework rests on three coined predicates, each naming a distinction the inherited governance vocabulary obscures by lumping it with something it isn't.

Sabbotarchy
sabotage + -archy

The regime form: rule by sabotage. The -archy suffix places the term in the etymological family of regime forms — monarchy, oligarchy, hierarchy, anarchy — where it always should have been.

Obfuscratic
obfuscation + -cratic

The operational signature: a regime operating through the deliberate production of confusion and manufactured incoherence. An adjective: it names how the regime operates, not what type it is.

Multimalocracy
multi- + mal- + -ocracy

The 42-mode discriminative-vocabulary taxonomy. Names the structural conditions Sabbotarchy operates within, alongside Imperocracy, Documentocracy, and the broader inherited apparatus.

The bipartite construction Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy is the framework's central commitment: rule-by-sabotage operating through deliberate obfuscation that frames the sabotage as incompetence.

Neither half is sufficient. A Sabbotarchy operating transparently would be recognized and resisted. An Obfuscratic regime without sabotage would have nothing to obfuscate. The compound names what actually happens.

VIIThe ludic substrate

The pattern, made playable.

Strata-Dom-3D is a research game in which the upstream-commitment pattern is exercised as gameplay. Players commit irrevocable plans in parallel. Reveals are simultaneous. Pre-committed weapons fire on schedule, in directions chosen at game start, with no opportunity to retract once the round resolves.

What the game lets you test is the same structural property the analytical instruments document: irrevocable commitment under mutual blindness, topological flanking, no-edge environments. The 32×12 toroidal battlefield is bounded but has no edge; pieces sliding along path-traversed lanes wrap continuously; weapon vectors fire across the topology and chain through whatever they meet. Same-type clashes resolve via skill duels — Precision Aim, Connect-Four, Memory, Cup-Shuffle — never by chance.

VIIIHow to use it

An iterative workflow across the two-instrument stack.

The instruments are designed for use together. An analyst with a question about a specific architecture follows this loop:

  1. Identify an operation of interest in the Catalysis Toroid. Click an actor or target; isolate its network at 1° depth; expand outward to 5° to see the multiplicative reach.
  2. Examine the operational cells (7.x) connected to that node. Read the INACT cells with particular care — those are where engineered incompetence is most legible, because each pairs an inaction with its documented downstream consequence.
  3. Trace the relevant R-cells (formal coordination) and B-cells (benefit-inferred wormholes). The B-cells often matter more than the R-cells for understanding sustainment across administrative transitions.
  4. Consult the Sabbotarchy framework for the signature score of the broader architecture the operation belongs to. Read the per-dimension breakdown — especially S5 (information-architecture asymmetry), which carries the highest weight.
  5. Confirm or revise the score on cell-specific evidence. Every scoring decision is anchored to documented evidence; every anchor is auditable; every score is revisable when new evidence emerges or interpretation is challenged.
  6. If wanted, exercise the upstream-commitment pattern in Strata-Dom-3D to test intuitions about pre-committed plans under simultaneous reveal — the structural property the instruments document analytically.

The framework is regime-character-agnostic with respect to political affiliation; it applies wherever the signature pattern emerges. Future versions will extend within the government-services domain (SSA, Department of Education, EPA, USDA, HUD, SEC architectures all warrant scoring) and beyond it. Cross-analyst replication is an open research program — the framework's external validity depends on whether independent analysts produce convergent rank-orderings of architectures.

It is a measurement procedure, not an accusation. It does not claim any specific architecture is engineered-incompetent; it provides the procedure by which an architecture can be measured on the signature dimensions and scored.

Naming Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy doesn't prove it; signature analysis does.
