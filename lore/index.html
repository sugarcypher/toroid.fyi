— Layer I · premise & vocabulary

A torus is the simplest surface that captures what an edge cannot. Bounded. Edgeless. Doubly periodic. Pressure on the north wraps to the south. Pressure on the east wraps to the west. There is no corner to retreat into.

Most game boards and most diagrams assume an edge. This one does not.

What strategies survive when you cannot retreat into an edge?

Each instrument here treats the torus as a research substrate, not a decoration. The relational catalog (Polymorphic Catalysis Toroid) maps which actors press on which targets through which intermediaries. The signature index (Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy) measures the five-dimensional shape of engineered incompetence. The playable substrate (Strata-Dom-3D) exercises the commitment loop as gameplay.

The framework's analytical predicates are neologisms — each names a distinction the standard vocabulary collapses.

Sabbotarchy
sabotage + -archy. The regime form: rule by sabotage.
Obfuscratic
obfuscation + -cratic. The operational signature: deliberate production of confusion and manufactured incoherence.
Multimalocracy
the 42-mode discriminative-vocabulary taxonomy within which Sabbotarchy operates.
