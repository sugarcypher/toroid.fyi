Why this instrument exists

The name. The Deception Arcane is this instrument's proper name — an arcane in the sense of a specialized diagnostic device: the signature instrument for diagnosing engineered-incompetence regimes. It is the measurement layer; the Polymorphic Catalysis Toroid (schema name: Convergence Topology) is the companion evidence layer its cell references point into.

Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy is the concept: rule by sabotage (the sabbotarchic regime form) operating through deliberate obfuscation that frames the sabotage as incompetence (the obfuscratic operational signature). Naming the regime is not sufficient — the concept cannot be proven by accusation. The diagnosis requires signature analysis. Genuine incompetence produces symmetric error, accepts correction, advances based on technical competence, and operates inside a coherent information architecture. Engineered incompetence produces asymmetric directional outcomes, resists correction disproportionately to its cost, advances loyalty over technical competence, and operates inside internal coherence flanked by manufactured external incoherence.

This instrument scores seven government-services-redirection architectures (six v0.1 seeds plus Regulatory Rollback, added v0.8 as a candidate) on the five signature dimensions. Each score is anchored to specific evidence — cells in the Polymorphic Catalysis Toroid, official documents, court filings, agency reports. A composite Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy Index emerges from the weighted aggregate. Architectures above 70 sit in the High-Asymmetry band — the pattern consistent with engineered incompetence; below 30 are consistent with genuine dysfunction or genuine competence; the band between requires further analysis. A note on the anchors: the toroid is the author's own documentation instrument — its cells are pointers into compiled public sources (documents, filings, records), not independent third-party verification; the primary sources behind each cell are what carry the evidentiary weight.

The instrument is a measurement apparatus, not an argument. The dedicated focus on government-services redirection is methodologically chosen — this is where asymmetric outcome distributions are most cleanly measurable and where the diagnostic case is structurally strongest. The redirection of funds from public-serving functions toward extraction, surveillance, and procurement is the cleanest available proof signal.

Temporal-dynamics layer

The comparison table above is a snapshot — each architecture's five-signature score at one moment. This layer adds time: it tracks the same five signatures across dated checkpoints to answer three questions the snapshot cannot.

How it connects: this is not a separate tool — it is the time-derivative of this instrument. It runs on the same five signatures and the same seven architectures in the comparison table above, and (once scored) on the candidate resource-operations dimensions S6–S8 documented in the methodology increments. The real-architecture table is the first one below; the abstract sandbox sits beneath it only to show the formulas compute.

In plain terms: (1) velocity — is an architecture's engineered-incompetence score rising, and how fast? A score of 80 reached slowly (an old, settled dysfunction) is not the same as 80 reached in a year (an active capture). (2) breadth-velocity — how quickly are new architectures crossing into the engineered band? This is the "everything at once" feeling, made into a number. (3) synchrony — when architectures cross the line, do they cross together? Coordinated programs cross in clusters; unrelated dysfunction crosses at random times. Synchrony is the one number here that can come out against the coordination claim — which is exactly why it's worth measuring.

Signature velocity
V = Σ wᵢ·(dSᵢ/dt)
Per-dimension and composite rate of change across dated epochs, in index-points per quarter. Positive = intensifying engineered signature; near-zero on a high score = entrenched equilibrium, not active capture.
Breadth-velocity
B(t) = ΔN₇₀ / Δt
Rate at which architectures cross the engineered threshold (≥70), against a fixed denominator. The formal version of "all levels at once." Valid only with the full scored frame in the denominator — the displayed N₇₀ uses the seven seeds plus the three scored reform-lineage baselines (unscored pre-registered controls join the frame when scored).
Onset synchrony
Σ = 1 − (σ_τ / σ_null)
Temporal concentration of threshold-crossings vs an independent-onset null. Σ→1: far more bunched than chance (coordination signal); Σ→0: indistinguishable from independent drift. Falsifiable — read only as excess over a historical transition baseline.

The seven architectures — longitudinal status (back-scored 2025-Q1 · 2025-Q3 · 2026-Q1)

The same seven architectures as the comparison table, re-scored on all five signatures at three dated checkpoints under as-of discipline (only evidence public by each date; audit trail in sabbotarchy_backscoring_evidence.md). Composite velocity is the change over the last interval; back-scoring was performed retrospectively, so as-of discipline restricts the evidence set, not the scorer's knowledge of outcomes — a limitation only prospective scoring removes. Synchrony caveat — read before citing Σ: DOGE enters the window already above 70 and the other six cross within the first interval, at the administration onset. Σ is therefore (1) confounded with ordinary fast regime change, and (2) window-dependent: against a uniform null over the full two-interval window Σ = +0.41, but against the single interval in which the crossings actually occur Σ = −0.17 — less bunched than chance. (The audit trail's Σ ≈ 0.37 was computed over six seeds; the displayed value recomputes over seven.) Σ is reported, not diagnostic. The reform-lineage baseline below shows mid-administration reform programs produce no crossings at all — but the diagnostic null Σ needs is other administration onsets (e.g. 1981, 2017, 2021), which is future work. Supporting agendas / related forces (doctrinal substrate — not scored)

Strategic doctrines, not architectures — not measured on the five signatures. They explain why engineered dysfunction is politically rewarded rather than punished: the layer that makes a sabbotarchy self-sustaining.

Two-Santa Claus Theory (Jude Wanniski, 1976)
In power, be the "tax-cut Santa" — cut taxes, never balance budgets; out of power, become the "spending-cut Santa," forcing the other party into austerity and tax increases. The deficit is the instrument, not the concern — the explicit ruin-then-blame ratchet.
Starve the Beast
Cut revenue first → manufacture deficits → invoke the deficits to demand spending cuts that could not pass on their own merits. The fiscal arm of the same ratchet.
The Shock Doctrine (Naomi Klein, 2007)
Use real or manufactured crises to push unpopular policy while the public is disoriented — the crisis-exploitation arm, and the theory behind the S6/S7/S8 resource operations.
Asymmetric deficit-hawkery (the ratchet)
Deficits expand in the tax-cut phase; "deficit concern" reappears only in opposition — the mechanism by which each cycle leaves the state smaller. The downstream effect the velocity / breadth metrics are measuring.

Analytic role: the five signatures measure what is done to each architecture; the resource-operations dimensions measure how value, force, and harm are moved; this layer explains why it persists. Explicitly outside the measured apparatus.

Methodology weights. Composite index is a weighted average of the five dimensions. Information-Architecture Asymmetry weighted highest (0.30) per the framework rationale: it is the cleanest operational definition — internal coherence inside the regime co-existing with manufactured external incoherence. Outcome-Distribution Asymmetry (0.25) — paired distributions of stated beneficiary vs. realized beneficiary. Domain-Selective Competence (0.20) — public-facing function failure-rate co-existing with extractive function success-rate. Anti-Remediation Response (0.15) — resistance intensity vs. cost of proposed fix. Recruitment Selection (0.10) — technical competence vs. loyalty advancement vs. dissent tolerance.

Index bands. 0–30 Genuine: consistent with genuine competence or genuine incompetence (low signature). 30–70 Mixed: further analysis required. 70–85 High-Asymmetry: satisfies the engineered-incompetence signature pattern. 85–100 Extreme-Asymmetry: signature concentration across all five dimensions — the measured pattern consistent with obfuscratic sabbotarchy; the band names state the measured property, and the verdict is left to the reader. (Bands renamed in v0.8 from "Engineered pattern / Extreme signature" — intent words removed from machine output.)

Robustness of the composite index — weight sensitivity. The 0.25/0.20/0.15/0.10/0.30 weights are justified narratively, so the rankings were stress-tested: under every ±0.05 single-pair weight shift (all 20 perturbations of the weight vector, one dimension −0.05 and another +0.05), each composite moves by at most ~1 point (IRS 90.1–92.0 · DOGE 89.0–89.8 · Rollback 87.5–88.3 · ICE 84.8–85.7 · OBBBA 84.2–86.0 · NIH 78.7–80.3 · VA 74.9–76.5). The ordering IRS > DOGE > Rollback > {ICE, OBBBA} > NIH > VA holds in all 20 perturbations with one exception: ICE and OBBBA sit 0.1 point apart and swap in 9 of 20 — so they are reported as a tie, not an ordering. Band membership is stable across all perturbations except OBBBA, which sits on the 85 boundary and drops from Extreme-Asymmetry to High-Asymmetry under 3 of 20 shifts. Extended sensitivity (v0.8): under radical re-weightings — S5 demoted to 0.10, all weights equal, and S5 dropped entirely (weights renormalized over S1–S4) — every architecture remains in the ≥70 High-Asymmetry band and the top-three order (IRS > DOGE > Rollback) is unchanged; OBBBA slips below the 85 line in some scenarios and the OBBBA/ICE tie shuffles. The band-level findings do not depend on S5's weight, or on S5 at all.

Robustness — band thresholds, corrected. Methodology paper v1.1 claimed the 70 boundary "corresponds to scoring in the engineered range on at least four of five dimensions" and 85 to all five. That correspondence does not survive arithmetic under unequal weights: four dimensions at exactly 70 with the fifth at 30 produce composites of 58–66 depending on which dimension is low, and a composite ≥85 is reachable without all five dimensions ≥70. The 70/85 thresholds are calibration conventions on the weighted mean — retained because the seed set's band membership is insensitive to the weight perturbations above — not dimension counts. The count-based justification is withdrawn.

Robustness — scoring uncertainty and range-valued evidence. Several S1 evidence figures are ranges (e.g., 9.9–15M projected coverage losses) and conversion of evidence to a 0–100 point involves analyst judgment. Treating per-dimension scores as ±5 points, the composite carries an error band of roughly ±2.4 (independent errors) to ±5 (fully correlated). Composite gaps inside ~5 points — DOGE 89 vs Rollback 88, ICE 85 vs OBBBA 85 — are therefore inside the scoring error band and are not asserted as ordinal claims. The load-bearing results are band membership and the seed-vs-baseline separation (seeds 76–91 vs reform-lineage controls 14–20), both of which sit far outside the error band.

Epistemics inventory (v0.8). Counted across the instrument's score-feeding data (~74 quantitative items at v0.4): ~7% carried explicit Occurred/Projected/Contested tags, ~11% a status adjective, ~82% no marker — while the non-index resource-operations layer was 100% tagged. The v0.8 pass added status markers and sources to the S1 magnitude fields and description figures above; the remaining untagged mass is the per-dimension sub-axis numbers (competence, retention, coherence values), which are analyst judgment scales, not measured percentages — no census of advancement decisions or dissent episodes exists, and they should be read as ordinal placements an experienced analyst could dispute by ±10 or more. They feed the dimension scores; the dimension scores are judgments; the honest unit of claim is the band. One instance of the tagging discipline running against the thesis, for the record: de-biasing the S6 need metric shrank the analyst's own headline inversion from ρ = −0.89 to −0.57 — the motivated-affirmation rule applied to the instrument's own finding.
