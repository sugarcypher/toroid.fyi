Why this instrument exists

Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy is the concept: rule by sabotage (the sabbotarchic regime form) operating through deliberate obfuscation that frames the sabotage as incompetence (the obfuscratic operational signature). Naming the regime is not sufficient — the concept cannot be proven by accusation. The diagnosis requires signature analysis. Genuine incompetence produces symmetric error, accepts correction, advances based on technical competence, and operates inside a coherent information architecture. Engineered incompetence produces asymmetric directional outcomes, resists correction disproportionately to its cost, advances loyalty over technical competence, and operates inside internal coherence flanked by manufactured external incoherence.

This instrument scores six government-services-redirection architectures on the five signature dimensions. Each score is anchored to specific evidence — cells in the Polymorphic Catalysis Toroid, official documents, court filings, agency reports. A composite Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy Index emerges from the weighted aggregate. Architectures above 70 satisfy the engineered-incompetence signature pattern; below 30 are consistent with genuine dysfunction or genuine competence; the band between requires further analysis.

The instrument is a measurement apparatus, not an argument. The dedicated focus on government-services redirection is methodologically chosen — this is where asymmetric outcome distributions are most cleanly measurable and where the diagnostic case is structurally strongest. The redirection of funds from public-serving functions toward extraction, surveillance, and procurement is the cleanest available proof signal.

Methodology weights. Composite index is a weighted average of the five dimensions. Information-Architecture Asymmetry weighted highest (0.30) per the framework rationale: it is the cleanest operational definition — internal coherence inside the regime co-existing with manufactured external incoherence. Outcome-Distribution Asymmetry (0.25) — paired distributions of stated beneficiary vs. realized beneficiary. Domain-Selective Competence (0.20) — public-facing function failure-rate co-existing with extractive function success-rate. Anti-Remediation Response (0.15) — resistance intensity vs. cost of proposed fix. Recruitment Selection (0.10) — technical competence vs. loyalty advancement vs. dissent tolerance.

Index bands. 0–30: consistent with genuine competence or genuine incompetence (low obfuscratic signature). 30–70: mixed signature; further analysis required. 70–85: satisfies engineered-incompetence signature pattern. 85–100: extreme signature concentration — obfuscratic sabbotarchy operating across all five dimensions.

Hybrid scoring methodology (v0.1). Six seed architectures are scored based on documented public evidence with full audit trail. Each score is traceable to specific evidence anchors (toroid cells, agency documents, court filings, congressional records, news reporting). The infrastructure supports extension to additional architectures via the same evidence-anchored methodology. Scores reflect documented operations as of May 2026.

Status. v0.1 ships with six seed architectures across the government-services-redirection domain. Future versions: extension to non-government architectures (corporate, foreign-state, civil-society), longitudinal scoring across time windows, automated evidence-extraction from toroid cells, per-architecture exportable reports with full citation chains, integration with the Errorfect DACRI protocol for cross-document substrate analysis.
