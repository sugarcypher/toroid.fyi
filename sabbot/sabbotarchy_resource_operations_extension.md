# The Resource-Operations Extension

**Redefining S6 and adding the manufactured-cost / coercion operations to the Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy framework**

*B. Greenway · ThinkWell Labs Metrology · methodology increment v0.3 · June 2026*

*Extends the methodology paper (v1.2), the velocity & synchrony extension (v0.2), the negative-control set (v0.2), and the evidence-anchor audit (v0.2). This increment reworks the proposed sixth dimension, separates three distinct operations that were collapsing into it, and annotates the existing S3 dimension with a new instance.*

---

## 14. Why this increment exists

S3 (anti-remediation) measures a **passive** cost operation: an existing dysfunction, resistant to correction even when the fix is cheap. In developing a candidate sixth dimension to capture the **active** inverse — operations that *generate* cost rather than protect existing dysfunction — a sequence of test cases revealed that "active cost generation for capture" is not one operation but several, each with a different resource moved and a different payoff currency. Forcing them into a single dimension broke repeatedly; each break was diagnostic. This increment separates them.

The development was case-driven, and the cases did the disciplinary work: some passed the candidate dimension, several failed it, and the failures — not the passes — forced the redefinition. That is the intended behavior of an instrument built to risk its author's prior rather than confirm it.

## 14.1 The originating critique: selection, not fabrication

The first draft of the sixth dimension ("Manufactured-Demand Extraction") gated on a single test: *was the problem fabricated?* A real-but-acted-upon crisis (the 2025 Argentina currency swap) appeared to fail the dimension because Argentina's crisis was genuine, not manufactured. But this is the wrong invariant, and the correction is the analytical core of this increment.

The world is saturated with genuine unmet needs — many of them domestic, many more severe than the ones acted upon. The diagnostic question is therefore not *did they fabricate this problem?* but **of all the genuine needs in the field, which ones get resources — and does that selection track need, or payoff?** A real crisis resourced because its beneficiary is aligned, while a greater crisis is ignored because no one cashes out, carries the *same* signature as a fabricated one. Fabrication is merely the limiting case in which the resourced "need" is approximately zero and the allocation asymmetry is maximal.

The invariant is **selection relative to need**, not fabrication. The dimension is redefined accordingly (14.3).

## 14.2 The four-operation typology of the resource axis

Separating the collapsed cases yields four distinct operations on the resource-and-power axis. One (S3) already exists in the framework; one (S6) is redefined here; two (S7, S8) are introduced as candidates.

| Dimension | Status | Mechanism | What is moved | Payoff currency | Anchor case |
|-----------|--------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **S3 — Anti-remediation** | existing (paper §3.3) | block / refuse an available fix | the fix itself | preservation of exploitable dysfunction | 2024 bipartisan border bill killed |
| **S6 — Selective-allocation asymmetry** | **redefined here** | resource aligned needs while skipping greater ones | money / relief / attention | financial capture + political alignment | Argentina swap; $1.8B self-settlement fund (manufactured special case) |
| **S7 — Asset/standing dissipation** | candidate | expend force, lives, and legal legitimacy | military assets, personnel, rule-of-law standing | political leverage + domestic spectacle | Caribbean/Pacific boat strikes; Maduro capture |
| **S8 — Induced-harm coercion** | candidate | create or deepen a crisis in a target population | the target population's welfare | geopolitical coercion | Cuba oil blockade; Venezuela pre-capture pressure |

The distinctions are not stylistic. Each operation moves a different resource and is paid in a different currency, and conflating them destroys measurability — most importantly by letting "capture" expand to mean *financial or political or spectacle or coercive* payoff, at which point every contested action qualifies and the instrument becomes the unfalsifiable accusation it is built to avoid. Keeping the four separate keeps each one falsifiable.

## 14.3 S6, redefined — Selective-Allocation Asymmetry

**Definition.** Resource deployment decoupled from, or inverted against, objective need, where the resourced cases share a capture/alignment payoff property and comparable-or-greater needs are systematically skipped.

**Measurement protocol.**

1. **Need-ranking.** Rank candidate crises (domestic and foreign) by objective severity, using external, pre-committed metrics — mortality, persons affected, cost-of-inaction — *not* analyst judgment.
2. **Allocation-ranking.** Rank where resources (money, relief, force, attention) actually went.
3. **Decoupling.** Measure the correlation between the two rankings. Genuine triage produces positive correlation (need is resourced). The signature is *negative* correlation — allocation inverted against need.
4. **Payoff property of the resourced set.** Test whether the resourced cases share a financial-capture or political-alignment property.
5. **Skip property of the ignored set.** Test whether the ignored cases share high need plus absence of payoff.

**Signature** = allocation *anti-correlates with need* **and** *correlates with payoff*. Manufactured demand (need ≈ 0, asymmetry maximal) is the special case, not the definition.

**Falsifiability guard (mandatory).** The signature is **not** "they failed to fix everything." Unmet needs always exist; partial response is ordinary triage and must score LOW. The signature exists only when allocation *inverts* against need while tracking payoff. Without this guard the dimension is unfalsifiable and must not be used.

**The soft joint — analyst bias enters at the need-ranking.** "Objective severity" is itself contestable, and an analyst can rig the ranking to manufacture an inversion. Two requirements follow: (a) the need-ranking must be built from external, pre-registered severity metrics, fixed before the allocation-ranking is examined; (b) any claim of "ignored greater needs" must be a named, ranked, anchored list, never a gesture at "countless ignored crises." Rhetoric is not measurement.

**Distinctness from S1 and S2.** S1 measures selectivity *within* a single operation (this program's stated vs. realized beneficiary). S2 measures competence selectivity *within* an architecture (public-facing failure vs. extractive success). Redefined-S6 measures selectivity *across the entire portfolio* — which problems get touched at all, against the field of those ignored. Per-operation vs. portfolio scope; no duplication.

**Required negative control.** A genuine-triage case — resources flowing to the most urgent/feasible needs, allocation positively correlated with need — predicted to score LOW. If it scores high, the need-ranking is rigged or the guard has failed.

## 14.4 S7 (candidate) — Asset/Standing Dissipation

**Definition.** Expenditure of national assets, personnel, lives, and legal legitimacy for a non-public, factional payoff (political leverage, regime change, domestic spectacle), under a manufactured or pretextual justification.

**Why separate from S6.** The resource moved is not money and the payoff is not financial capture. S6's "follow the money" does not apply; S7 is "follow the power and the bodies." Folding it into S6 would require broadening "capture" to swallow political payoff, which breaks falsifiability.

**Four-part test.** (1) Manufactured/pretextual justification? (2) Assets/lives/legality expended? (3) Net public value low against the stated aim? (4) Factional/political payoff present? High S7 requires all four.

**Required negative control.** A genuine national-security action — real threat, proportionate force, lawful authorization, strategic public benefit — predicted to score LOW.

**Exposure note.** S7 is the most intent-exposed dimension in the framework. National-security actions carry defensible public rationales, so scoring one as engineered must rest on *documented* gaps (ineffectiveness against the stated aim, absence of authorization, civilian victims, manufactured pretext), never on asserted venal intent. This is the easiest place for the instrument to be caught reading intent into events, and must be scored most conservatively.

## 14.5 S8 (candidate) — Induced-Harm Coercion

**Definition.** Deliberate creation or deepening of a humanitarian crisis in a target population, used as a coercion lever. The resource moved is the target population's welfare; the payoff is geopolitical leverage.

**Why separate.** This is the mirror image of S6's selective *give*: a selective *deny/destroy*. Neither extraction (S6) nor force-expenditure (S7) captures it — the operation's product is suffering, deployed as pressure.

**Four-part test.** (1) Is the harm caused or materially deepened by the actor's deliberate action (not merely the target's own failures)? (2) Does the harm fall on a civilian/non-combatant population? (3) Is it instrumentalized — tied to a coercive demand or leverage objective? (4) Obfuscated — framed as something other than what it is (sanctions "for security," etc.)?

**Required negative control.** A targeted sanction with documented carve-outs for food and medicine and no measurable civilian-mortality effect — predicted to score LOW. The signature is the *removal* of such carve-outs and the resulting population-level harm.

**Causal-attribution discipline (mandatory).** Induced-harm claims live or die on causation, and causation is where motivated reasoning enters from both directions. The actor's action being a *major proximate cause* must be distinguished from it being the *sole cause*; the target's own economic and governance failures are typically real co-factors. Score the documented causal contribution, not a totalizing one.

## 14.6 S3 annotation — the blocked fix as anti-remediation

The immigration case adds a clean instance to the *existing* S3 dimension, anchored to seed architecture #3 (ICE/DHS detention, toroid cell **B040 — "ICE/DHS ↔ private-equity detention architecture"**).

S3 measures resistance to correction disproportionate to the cost of the fix. The February 2024 bipartisan border bill (Lankford–Murphy–Sinema) is a near-ideal instance: a fix was not merely available but was the conservative bill its own side had demanded — its Republican author called it "by far the most conservative border security bill in four decades" — and it was killed after the party was told to block it so the crisis would remain a campaign issue ("blame it on me"). The cost of the fix was negative (it delivered the demanded policy); resistance was maximal. That is the inverted cost-resistance slope S3 is built to detect, and it is the datum the "genuine policy disagreement" alternative explanation cannot absorb: a side obtained the bill it demanded and destroyed it for political utility.

This S3 instance should be recorded against B040, and it is the upstream link in the immigration chain (14.7).

## 14.7 Chaining across operations

The dimensions are not independent; their diagnostic power is amplified when they chain on a single target.

- **Immigration chain: S3 → S6.** Block every available fix (S3; 2024 bill) → the sustained crisis justifies enforcement and detention spending → captured by private-detention contractors (S6, financial; B040). Anti-remediation manufactures the demand that selective-allocation/extraction then feeds on.
- **Venezuela chain: S8 → S7 → S6.** Pre-capture pressure and crisis-induction (S8) → kinetic campaign under the "narco-boat" pretext and the capture of Maduro (S7) → post-capture oil routed into "U.S.-controlled accounts" (S6, financial). Coercion sets up force; force enables extraction.

Cross-operation chaining on a single target is the meta-signature the framework was built to surface: it is far harder to explain as a sequence of independent good-faith policy choices than any single operation in isolation.

## 14.8 Worked anchors (with status tags)

Each anchor is tagged Occurred / Projected / Contested per the evidence-anchor audit discipline (increment v0.2, §13). Intent and causation claims are tagged separately from the events themselves.

**$1.776B "Anti-Weaponization Fund" / IRS self-settlement — S6, passing (manufactured special case). [Revised after second neutral anchor.]**
*Occurred (neutral-sourced — DOJ release, NBC, Wikipedia, Time):* in January 2026 Trump, his sons, and the Trump Org sued the IRS/Treasury for $10B over the leak of his tax returns; the suit was settled by his own DOJ (acting AG Blanche, his former personal lawyer). Per the settlement, plaintiffs receive **a formal apology and no monetary damages**, drop the suit with prejudice, and withdraw further claims (Mar-a-Lago raid, "Russia-collusion"); and — the durable secured benefit — the **IRS is "forever barred" from auditing past returns of Trump, his family, or their companies.** The DOJ announced a $1.776B "Anti-Weaponization Fund" alongside the settlement.
*Contested / status-in-flux:* the fund itself — Acting AG Blanche testified (~June 3 2026) the DOJ would **not move forward** with it, while Sen. Coons' office (June 5 2026) treated payouts to Jan-6 assailants/family/donors as a live risk and a barring amendment failed 54–45. So the cash-payout/"slush fund" reading is **contested and possibly cancelled**, not occurred.
*Why it still passes S6 — re-grounded:* the signature does not depend on the fund being funded. The manufactured cost is the **self-generated litigation → self-settlement** (sue your own IRS, settle with your own DOJ), and the realized capture is the **audit-bar** — a concrete, durable, self-dealing benefit secured regardless of any payout. Obfuscatory "anti-weaponization / victims" framing over a self-settlement. *Lesson logged:* the second neutral anchor weakened the payout framing (apology, no damages; DOJ won't proceed) and strengthened the anchor by relocating it onto the documented settlement terms — exactly the revision the second-anchor discipline is meant to force.

**Argentina $20B currency swap — S6, signal via redefinition (real crisis, selected by alignment).**
*Occurred:* $20B Exchange Stabilization Fund swap, Oct 9 2025, ~$2.5B drawn, conditioned by Trump on Milei's party's electoral success; no U.S. military action in Argentina. **Repayment now independently corroborated** (Reuters, Bloomberg, Buenos Aires Herald, UPI; Argentina's central bank settled the ~$2.5B drawn in December 2025), upgrading it from interested-source to occurred; only the "tens of millions in profit" gloss remains Bessent's characterization (contested). *Under the old definition* Argentina failed (not fabricated). *Under redefined-S6* it is signal: a real but non-exceptional crisis resourced because the beneficiary is aligned (and investors with Argentine holdings benefit), while greater needs go unresourced. Note it still fails the *fabrication* special-case test (test 1 of the old draft) — repayment affects only net-value, not selection — so it scores on selective-allocation, not on manufacture.

**Caribbean/Pacific boat strikes — S7 (asset/standing dissipation), not S6.**
*Occurred:* strikes since Sept 2025, 200+ killed, 60+ boats; AP identified some dead as fishermen earning ~$500/trip; the campaign targets cocaine routes while fentanyl (the overdose driver) enters overland from Mexico; Coast Guard had set a 2024 cocaine-seizure record; first-strike survivors were killed in a follow-up; no congressional authorization; Amnesty and lawsuits allege extrajudicial killing / war crimes. *Contested:* purpose/intent. *Why S7 not S6:* no documented financial capture; assets/lives/legality expended for political/strategic payoff (Venezuela pressure → Maduro capture). Gravest case on the board; score on documented effectiveness/legality gaps only.

**Venezuela — S8 → S7 → S6 chain.**
*Occurred:* military buildup and boat strikes (S7) → Operation Absolute Resolve captured Maduro Jan 3 2026 (S7) → Jan 6 sanctions lifted, 50-million-barrel oil deal, Venezuelan oil proceeds routed into "U.S.-controlled accounts" (~$1B realized, ~$5B projected) (S6, financial). *Correction:* "bailing out Venezuela" is inverted — there is no bailout; the flow is *extraction from* Venezuela. Retag from "bailout" to "post-capture resource extraction."

**Cuba oil blockade — S8 (induced-harm coercion).**
*Occurred:* U.S. began blocking oil tankers to Cuba Feb 2026 (targeting Pemex et al. with tariff threats); quantified pediatric toll — ~11,000 children awaiting suspended surgeries, infant mortality up to 9.9/1,000, childhood-cancer survival down to 65%, medicine at ~30%, food production −60%, newborns hand-ventilated through blackouts; UN human-rights chief warns children are dying. *Contested:* causal share — U.S. action is well-supported as a *major proximate* cause; *sole* cause is contested (Cuba's own economic/governance failures are co-factors). Score the documented contribution. (Le Monde origin link was inaccessible; corroborated via UN News, Think Global Health, ABC News, Wikipedia's "2026 Cuban crisis.")

**Immigration — S3 → S6 chain (see 14.6, 14.7).**
*Occurred:* 2013 S.744 passed the Senate and died in the House; 2014 House rollback vote (context only — partisan, decade-old, prior administration); 2024 bipartisan border bill killed after Trump urged rejection. *Overreach to reject:* "the *entire* immigration atrocity is created" — the enforcement architecture and its engineered dysfunction are anchorable; *migration itself* (driven by violence, economics, climate in source countries) is not U.S.-manufactured. Split the defensible claim from the totalizing one or both fall.

## 14.9 Integrity constraints carried from prior increments

- **Candidate status.** S6-redefined, S7, and S8 are **candidate dimensions**. None enters the composite Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy Index or alters the existing weights until its negative control validates it. The instrument's headline composite remains the five-dimension v0.1/v1.2 index.
- **Contestation-quality filter (from §13.2).** Motivated denial does not downgrade a documented anchor; motivated affirmation does not upgrade a contested intent- or causation-claim. The asymmetry runs both ways and applies with full force to the politically charged cases here.
- **Tag separation.** Events are tagged Occurred/Projected/Contested independently of the intent and causation claims attached to them. An event occurring and the event being an instance of an engineered operation are two different rows.
- **Dated anchors.** Every anchor carries a date, as required for both the evidence audit and longitudinal/velocity scoring.

## 14.10 Status and next steps

1. **Negative controls** must be written and pre-registered for each of S6-redefined, S7, and S8 before any is scored (genuine triage; genuine national-security action; carve-out-preserving targeted sanction).
2. **The need-ranking metric for S6** must be specified from external sources and pre-committed before any architecture is scored on it.
3. **B040 (ICE/DHS) should carry an S3 annotation** recording the 2024 blocked border bill as anti-remediation evidence.
4. **Second neutral anchors** are needed for the partisan-sourced cases (the $1.8B fund) and for the interested-source claims (Argentina repayment).
5. These three candidate dimensions, once controlled, are the natural inputs to the **breadth-velocity denominator and synchrony index** (velocity extension §11) — the resource-axis operations are where cross-architecture chaining is most visible over time.
