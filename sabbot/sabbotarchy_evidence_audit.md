# Evidence-Anchor Audit — Six Seed Architectures

**Status-tagging of the v0.1 evidence anchors, with a contestation-quality filter**

*B. Greenway · ThinkWell Labs Metrology · methodology increment v0.2 · June 2026*

*Audits the factual anchors underlying the six seed-architecture scores. Each anchor is tagged Occurred / Projected / Contested. Contestation is weighted by evidentiary quality, not by its mere existence.*

---

## 13. Why the anchors must be audited

The methodology paper grounds the framework's authority in its documentation: "the framework's authority is derivative of the documentation behind it, not constitutive of any analyst's claim" (§10). That claim is only honoured if the documentation survives inspection. The single most common way a signature instrument fails an adversarial reviewer is **projection-stated-as-realized** — a modeled or forecast outcome (a CBO ten-year estimate, a coverage-loss projection) written into an S1 outcome-distribution row as though it had already occurred. One such substitution voids the row regardless of how clean the surrounding mathematics is. This audit finds the anchors carrying that exposure and tags every anchor so the exposure is explicit rather than buried.

## 13.1 The three status tags

- **Occurred** — a documented event that has taken place, verifiable against the contemporaneous record (statute, court filing, agency notice, contemporaneous reporting). Eligible to anchor a *realized*-outcome scoring claim.
- **Projected** — a modeled, forecast, or estimated future outcome (e.g., a CBO/CBPP coverage estimate with a future horizon). Legitimate evidence, but may anchor only a *projected*-outcome claim, and must be labeled as such in the S1 row.
- **Contested** — the magnitude, attribution, or characterization is disputed. The tag does **not** by itself downgrade the claim; whether it lowers confidence depends on the *quality* of the contestation (§13.2).

## 13.2 The contestation-quality filter

A claim's status is set by the documentary record and the **quality-weighted** balance of contestation — never by the bare existence of contestation. This guard exists because motivated denial is cheap and abundant; if the mere presence of a dispute downgraded a claim, every well-documented fact could be neutralized by an interested party simply asserting otherwise. The filter sorts contestation into two classes:

**High-quality contestation (adjusts confidence):** methodological critique; alternative measurements from credible, disinterested sources; timeframe/base-rate corrections (the projection-vs-realized distinction is the canonical case); scope or attribution corrections. These are evidence and are weighed as such.

**Low-quality / motivated contestation (does NOT downgrade):** bare denial by an interested party contradicting the documentary record; reframing without counter-evidence; ad hominem; assertion of intent in place of fact. These are logged but carry no evidentiary weight against a documented anchor.

The asymmetry is deliberate and runs in both directions: it prevents motivated denial from voiding a solid anchor, and it equally prevents motivated *affirmation* from rescuing a weak one. A documented cut denied by an official whose program enacted it is not thereby made uncertain; a contested magnitude is not made certain because the analyst finds it congenial.

## 13.3 Anchor-by-anchor findings

Verification conducted June 2026 against the contemporaneous public record. Each finding gives the as-stated v0.1 claim, the verified status, and the resulting recommendation.

### IG firings (S3 anchor — anti-remediation)
*As stated:* 17 inspectors general fired Jan 24–25 2025; reinstatement is effectively zero-cost yet categorically resisted → unbounded cost-resistance ratio.
*Verified:* **Occurred.** ≥17 IGs fired Jan 24 2025 without the statutory 30-day congressional notice. **Nuance requiring a verbiage fix:** on Sept 24 2025 a federal court ruled the firings unlawful but *declined to reinstate*, noting the President could re-fire after providing the mandated 30 days' notice. The "zero-cost reinstatement categorically resisted" framing is therefore complicated — a court, not only the regime, declined reinstatement, and did so on a statutory-procedure ground. *Recommendation:* retain the anchor but re-state the S3 example to acknowledge the judicial posture; the unbounded-ratio claim as written overreaches the record.

### IRS Direct File termination (S2 anchor — domain-selective competence)
*As stated:* termination of a working publicly-funded free filing tool co-existing with retained EITC audit capacity against low-income filers.
*Verified:* **Occurred.** IRS notified states Direct File "will not be available in Filing Season 2026" (formalized Nov 2025; consistent with the paper's May 2026 date). Corroborating the S2 reading: ~300,000 users in 25 states for TY2024; 94% of 2025 users rated the experience excellent/above-average; GAO deemed the pilot a success. A working, high-satisfaction public-facing tool was ended. *Recommendation:* **strongest anchor in the set; no change.**

### IRS probationary terminations (S4 anchor — recruitment selection)
*As stated:* ~6,000 IRS probationary employees terminated Feb 2025, targeting technical-competence early-career staff.
*Verified:* **Occurred, with a material nuance.** 7,315 probationary employees sent termination notices Feb 20 2025; TIGTA later found the IRS terminated them *without regard to performance.* But Maryland courts ordered reinstatement by Mar 17 2025; all 7,315 were reinstated; net reduction then occurred via the deferred-resignation program (~3,500) and resignations (~750), with ~3,000 actually returning. *Recommendation:* retain; adjust the S4 wording so the headcount reduction is attributed to the *reinstatement-then-attrition* path, not to the initial firing alone. TIGTA's "ignored performance" finding supports the selection claim but is subtly different from "targeted the competent" — the former is documented, the latter is an inference; state the documented version.

### Schedule F / Schedule Policy/Career (S4 anchor — institutional)
*As stated:* Schedule F converts career civil-service positions into political appointments, enabling replacement of technical staff with loyalty-pipeline candidates.
*Verified:* **Occurred as mechanism; scale Projected.** EO "Restoring Accountability to Policy-Influencing Positions" signed Jan 20 2025; OPM final rule finalized Feb 2026; OPM estimates ~50,000 positions (~2% of the workforce) reclassified; the rule removes MSPB appeal and statutory whistleblower protections. The *architecture* exists and is documented; *mass conversion of named incumbents* had not fully executed as of early 2026. *Recommendation:* retain as an S4 institutional anchor; label the architecture **Occurred** and the realized-replacement scale **Projected**.

### OBBBA / Medicaid coverage losses (S1 anchor — outcome-distribution asymmetry)
*As stated:* "realized outcome is approximately $1.3 trillion in upward wealth transfer co-existing with 10.9–15 million in coverage losses producing documented downstream mortality."
*Verified:* **Projected, not Occurred — this is the framework's principal exposure.** The 10.9–15M figure corresponds to CBO/CBPP estimates with a **2034 horizon** (CBPP 9.9–14.9M at risk; CBO ~15M including ACA-marketplace effects; Medicaid cut >$900B–$1T over the decade). These are forecasts, not realized counts. "Documented downstream mortality" is a **modeled projection**, not a recorded death toll. *Low-quality contestation present and correctly discountable:* an HHS official publicly denied the OBBBA Medicaid cuts; FactCheck.org documented the denial as contrary to the statute — this is motivated denial and does **not** downgrade the existence of the cuts. *High-quality contestation that DOES apply:* the realized-vs-projected timeframe correction. *Recommendation (verbiage fix, non-optional):* reclassify the S1 coverage and mortality claims from *realized* to *projected (CBO/CBPP, 2034 horizon)*. If S1 explicitly admits projected outcomes as scoring evidence — which is defensible — OBBBA's score need not fall; but the row must say "projected," or an adversarial reviewer voids it on the realized-as-stated ground alone.

### OBBBA upward-transfer magnitude ($1.3T) (S1 anchor)
*As stated:* "approximately $1.3 trillion in upward wealth transfer."
*Verified:* **Contested / Constructed.** $1.3T is not a single attributable CBO/JCT figure; it is an analyst aggregate. The verifiable neighboring figures: ~$4.5T total tax cuts over the decade; ~$2.3T of provisions delivering most benefit to the top 10%; ~$1T cost of the top-1% cuts specifically; CBO distributional finding that the bottom decile *loses* ~3.1% of income while the top decile gains ~2.7% by 2034. *Recommendation (verbiage fix):* replace the unanchored "$1.3 trillion" with a sourced figure — e.g., "~$2.3T of provisions accrue mainly to the top income decile (CBO/JCT distributional analysis, 2034 horizon)" — which is both verifiable and directionally stronger for the S1 asymmetry claim than the constructed number.

### DOGE federal workforce reduction (S1/S5 anchors)
*As stated:* composite 89; extreme S1 + S5.
*Verified:* **Occurred (existence and operation); specific composite-driving magnitudes require their own dated anchors.** DOGE's existence and its role in driving 2025 federal workforce reductions are well documented; the particular numerical anchors that drive the 89 were not individually re-verified in this pass. *Recommendation:* schedule a dedicated anchor-verification pass for the DOGE row before it is used in any longitudinal/velocity series; tag pending items **Contested-pending** until dated.

## 13.4 Summary of required verbiage corrections

The audit yields four corrections to the canonical methodology paper, all in the direction of *tightening claims to the record* — none weakening the framework's structure, all strengthening its defensibility:

1. **OBBBA S1 (§3.1):** reclassify coverage-loss and downstream-mortality outcomes from *realized* to *projected (CBO/CBPP, 2034 horizon)*.
2. **OBBBA magnitude (§3.1):** replace "$1.3 trillion" with the sourced ~$2.3T-to-top-decile figure (CBO/JCT distributional analysis).
3. **IG firings / S3 (§3.3):** acknowledge the Sept 2025 judicial posture; soften the "unbounded cost-resistance ratio" to reflect that a court declined reinstatement on statutory-procedure grounds.
4. **IRS probationary / S4 (§3.4):** attribute the net headcount reduction to the reinstatement-then-attrition path and state TIGTA's documented "ignored performance" finding rather than the stronger "targeted the competent" inference.

A fifth, cross-cutting recommendation: **every anchor acquires a date and a status tag**, which the velocity extension requires independently for as-of-epoch back-scoring. The audit and the velocity layer thus converge on one discipline — dated, tagged anchors — and that discipline is what lets the instrument keep its claim to be measurement rather than accusation.
