# The Negative-Control Set

**Pre-registered discrimination test for the Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy signature framework**

*B. Greenway · ThinkWell Labs Metrology · methodology increment v0.2 · June 2026*

*Companion to the methodology paper (v1.1) and the Deception Arcane instrument (v0.1). This document pre-registers architectures predicted to score LOW or MIXED, before evidence-anchored scoring, to test whether the instrument discriminates rather than merely confirms.*

## 12. The problem this set exists to close

The v0.1 instrument scored six architectures, all drawn from the government-services-redirection domain, all selected (paper §6) "because they were predicted to display the signature pattern most cleanly," and all returned composites of 74–89. The methodology paper then treats that clustering as meta-level evidence of coordination (§10). This is the framework's single gravest methodological vulnerability: **selection on the dependent variable.** If the only architectures admitted to the instrument are ones chosen because they were expected to score high, then their scoring high is an artifact of the selection rule, not a finding about the world — and the instrument, as deployed, cannot return a low score because nothing capable of returning one has been entered into it. An instrument that cannot fail is not a measurement device; it is the rhetorical accusation the framework explicitly claims not to be.

The fix is not more confirming cases. It is a set of **negative controls** — architectures scored by the identical rubric, whose scores are *predicted in advance* to land low or mixed. The controls do three things the seed set cannot:

1. They prove the instrument *can* return a low score (it has a working zero).
2. They test each dimension's discriminating power by isolating cases that are genuinely dysfunctional but lack the engineered *signature*.
3. They supply the fixed denominator and the historical transition baseline that the velocity extension's breadth-velocity and synchrony index require.

## 12.1 Pre-registration discipline

Pre-registration is the methodological core of this document, and its integrity depends on sequence. The predicted band and predicted per-dimension pattern for each control are **registered here, before evidence-anchored scoring.** The indicative scores in §12.4 are explicitly marked as first-pass and low-confidence; formal scoring — by the full evidence-anchored rubric, ideally by multiple analysts blind to these predictions — is the registered next step. The value of the exercise is destroyed if predictions are revised after scoring to match outcomes. The predictions are therefore locked in this version and any later revision must be logged as a dated amendment, not a silent edit.

## 12.2 Control-selection principles

A useful negative control must be **plausibly confusable** with an engineered case — genuinely dysfunctional, large, consequential — so that a low score is a real discrimination and not a trivial one. A control that is obviously benign tests nothing. Each control below is therefore a real instance of state dysfunction or large-scale restructuring; the prediction that it scores low rests on the *absence of the signature*, not the absence of dysfunction. The set deliberately spans:

- the genuine band (0–30), to anchor the scale's floor;
- the mixed band (30–70), to populate the middle the seed set leaves empty;
- at least one case of a **political valence opposite** to the seed architectures, scored by the same rubric, to test the framework's claimed political-agnosticism and to directly answer the cherry-picking critique.

## 12.3 The pre-registered controls

### C1 — Chronically underfunded program failing symmetrically (Indian Health Service, sustained underfunding)

*Predicted band:* **Genuine (0–30).**
*Rationale / predicted pattern:* Catastrophic public-facing failure (access, wait-times, outcomes) **co-occurring with no surgical extractive counter-function** — the decisive contrast with the seed cases. S2 should score low because both function-classes fail together; there is no "competence against" flanking the "buffoonery for." S3 should show a *positive* cost-resistance slope: remediation is resisted in proportion to its cost, because the constraint is real scarcity, not structural protection. S1 should be near-symmetric: the intended beneficiary and the failed-to-be-served population are the same. *Tests:* S2 and S3 discrimination. *Falsification trigger:* if C1 scores ≥70, S2/S3 are firing on dysfunction-magnitude rather than on the signature, and both dimensions are confounded.

### C2 — Prior-administration mass workforce reduction (Clinton–Gore "Reinventing Government" / National Partnership for Reinventing Government, 1993–2000)

*Predicted band:* **Genuine to low-Mixed (predicted < 35).**
*Rationale / predicted pattern:* This is the **most important control in the set**, because it is structurally the closest analogue to a seed case (DOGE) — a large federal workforce reduction (~426,000 positions over the period) executed as a deliberate program — while being predicted to score low. The prediction rests on signature-absence: S1 near-symmetric (savings publicly returned to deficit reduction and reinvestment, no identified extraction architecture as realized beneficiary); S5 *high external coherence* (the program was openly documented, GAO-tracked, publicly reported — the opposite of manufactured external incoherence). *Tests:* whether S1 and S5 key on the *signature* or merely on *workforce reduction as a policy direction.* *Falsification trigger:* if C2 scores in the engineered band (≥70), the instrument is keying on "large workforce cut" rather than on engineered incompetence, and the seed-architecture scores driven by S1/S5 are correspondingly suspect. **This is the cleanest single test of the whole framework's validity.**

### C3 — Genuinely botched rollout with no extraction (HealthCare.gov launch failure, 2013)

*Predicted band:* **Genuine (0–30).**
*Rationale / predicted pattern:* A catastrophic, nationally visible public-facing failure — the kind of conspicuous incompetence the framework is designed to discriminate from *engineered* incompetence. Predicted low because: S1 symmetric (the intended beneficiaries were the failed-to-be-served; no counter-party captured value from the failure); S3 strongly *positive/non-protective* (the architecture spent heavily and fast to *fix* the failure — the precise inverse of anti-remediation); S5 symmetric-low (the dysfunction was openly acknowledged, not obfuscated). *Tests:* the S3 anti-remediation inversion specifically — a genuine architecture *races to remediate*, the engineered one protects the dysfunction. *Falsification trigger:* if C3's S3 scores high (resistance to remediation), the S3 construct is not capturing what it claims to.

### C4 — Opposite-valence policy scored by the same rubric (a program the analyst is plausibly sympathetic to — e.g., a federal eviction-moratorium or student-debt-relief implementation)

*Predicted band:* **Mixed (30–70), wherever the evidence lands — the prediction is deliberately not pinned low.**
*Rationale / predicted pattern:* The point of C4 is **not** its score; it is the analyst's binding commitment to score a politically sympathetic architecture by the identical rubric, with the identical evidence discipline. If such a program exhibits outcome-distribution asymmetry or domain-selective competence, the rubric must register it. *Tests:* analyst-bias detectability and the framework's political-agnosticism claim. *Falsification trigger:* if structurally comparable cases score systematically lower when the analyst is sympathetic than when hostile, *given comparable evidence*, the instrument is measuring analyst valence and requires blinded, multi-analyst scoring before any of its outputs can be trusted. C4 is the direct, on-the-record answer to the cherry-picking charge.

### C5 — A well-functioning routine architecture (a high-performing agency function in a stable delivery period)

*Predicted band:* **Genuine, scale floor (0–15).**
*Rationale / predicted pattern:* Symmetric outcomes, uniform competence across function-classes, positive cost-resistance slope, balanced recruitment, symmetric internal/external coherence. *Tests:* that the instrument has a functioning floor and does not pin a benign case in the mixed band by construction. *Falsification trigger:* if a genuinely well-functioning architecture scores above ~30, the instrument's baseline is miscalibrated and every score sits on an inflated zero.

### C6 — (Optional) Cross-domain high-scoring positive control of non-US-administration character

*Predicted band:* **Engineered (≥70), different valence/domain.**
*Rationale:* A corporate or foreign-state architecture predicted high would demonstrate that engineered scores are not an artifact specific to one US administration. Listed for completeness; outside the immediate discrimination test and deferred to a later increment.

## 12.5 The collective falsification logic

The set is constructed so that the framework can lose. If the predicted-genuine controls (C1, C3, C5) score genuine and the structural analogue (C2) scores low while the seed architectures score high *under identical rubric application*, the instrument has demonstrated discrimination: it distinguishes engineered incompetence from genuine dysfunction, from prior-administration restructuring, and from honest botched rollouts. That is the result that would license the seed-architecture scores. If instead the controls score high — particularly C2 — the instrument is shown to be keying on policy-direction or dysfunction-magnitude rather than on the engineered signature, and the seed scores are invalidated to the same degree. Either outcome is informative; only the *possibility* of the second outcome makes the first one worth anything.

This is what converts the framework from an instrument that confirms its author's prior into one that risks it. The negative-control set is, deliberately, the part of the apparatus most capable of embarrassing its own author — which is the property a fortress of thought is built around, not against.
