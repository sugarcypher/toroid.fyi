Polarity tensions — P-cells documenting opposing pressures within the operating coalition

Where R-cells document coordination and B-cells document benefit-inferred backchannels, P-cells document POLARITY OPPOSITION: actors operating within the same broader operating coalition whose theoretical and aesthetic priors are oppositional, even where their operational effects converge. The two poles produce tension dynamics that the schema can document analytically.

Wormholes — B-cells documenting benefit-inferred backchannel relationships

Where R-cells require documented evidence of a connection (alliance, ownership, staffing, funding, institutional embedding), B-cells document the proof-of-benefit relationship that exists despite the absence or insufficiency of documented proof-of-connection. The analytical move: if Actor A's actions consistently produce benefit for Actor B (and vice versa) in ways that suggest backchannel alignment, this relationship is analytically real even if its mechanism is undocumented.

This is methodologically distinct from speculation. B-cells require:

  1. Documented benefit accruing to each party from the relationship
  2. Sustained pattern over time (not single coincidence)
  3. Structural incentive for the relationship to remain undocumented

B-cells render as wormhole tubes with magenta-cyan dual-color treatment matching the chromatic-aberration register of the nested-torus wireframes. The helical geometry signals "shortcut through hyperspace" — connecting two nodes via a topologically distinct path that does not appear in the standard relationship architecture.

Direction codes:

  • MUTUAL — both parties benefit from the relationship
  • A_BEN — primarily Actor A benefits
  • B_BEN — primarily Actor B benefits

Equatorial bisection: circulatory flow encoding

The nested-torus geometry is bisected at the equator. The bisection encodes the circulatory model that the predecessor Polymorphic Matrix was built on: structural pressure does not operate as one-directional cause-and-effect but as a circulatory dynamic where origination, implementation, consequence, and feedback compound over time.

Upper hemisphere — origination. Theory production (Heritage, Federalist Society, Claremont, CNP), capital provision (asset managers, private equity, family offices, sovereign-wealth funds), ideology sourcing (religious-political networks, Eurasianism, Yarvin-circle neo-monarchism, tech-accelerationism), governance-template export (Hungarian Orbán model, Argentine Milei state-shrinking), AI-substrate provision (foundation model providers, Big Tech, data brokers). The upper hemisphere atmospheric treatment is cold steel-blue — analytically denoting the ordered, deliberate, multi-decade institutional infrastructure where pressure is sourced.

Lower hemisphere — consequence. Implementation-instrument actors (ICE-DHS, DOGE-OPM, defense-tech contractors, commercial spyware, non-state armed and cyber proxies); all institutions under capture or capacity loss (federal agencies under workforce reduction, democratic infrastructure under pressure, environmental targets under regulatory rollback); all populations bearing direct harm (Medicaid populations, immigrants, climate-vulnerable populations, trans youth, journalists, indigenous nations). The lower hemisphere atmospheric treatment is warm rust-red — analytically denoting the entropic, mortality-bearing, time-lagged consequence-accumulation where pressure compounds into measurable harm.

Equator — transformation boundary. Marked by prominent silver rings at theta=0 (outermost equator) and theta=π (innermost equator) on both outer and inner tori. This is the analytical-significance plane where origination becomes implementation and implementation becomes consequence. Nodes positioned on the equator (boundary classification) are simultaneously originator-and-instrument — Treasury operating as both monetary-architecture upstream and sanctions-implementation downstream; constitutional structures operating as both foundational infrastructure and contested terrain; Big Tech platforms operating as both substrate-source and platform-instrument.

Cross-equator flow. Cells where the actor is upper-hemisphere and the target is lower-hemisphere render with directional flow particles (toggleable via FLOW button). The particles travel from origination to consequence, making the directional dynamic visually legible. Cells fully within a single hemisphere (upper-to-upper R-cells documenting apparatus-internal architecture, lower-to-lower edges documenting downstream-on-downstream pressure cascades) do not carry directional indicators because they represent intra-hemisphere structural relationships rather than transformation crossings.

Feedback loop through center. The convergence node at the center of the nested torus is the structural attractor through which consequence-effects feed back into origination dynamics. Mortality among Medicaid populations becomes political ammunition for further consolidation. Immigration enforcement spectacle generates electoral coalition consolidation. Substrate capture compounds over time because downstream effects generate upstream resources. The feedback mechanism is what distinguishes circulatory analysis from one-directional causal analysis — and it's why structural pressure intensifies rather than dissipating over time. The convergence node's pulsing animation continues to visualize this attractor function.

Actor-to-Actor Relationships (R-cells)

The bipartite cellular schema (7.x cells) documents actors operating against targets. The R-cells document actors operating with, through, under, owning, funding, or embedded within other actors — the operating-coalition's internal structural architecture.

Each R-cell has three identifiers:

  • R-number (R001–R130): stable numerical identifier
  • Code-name (e.g., Pyrite-Anvil, Slate-Garrote): opaque two-word compound drawing from disparate lexical fields — mineralogy, fluvial geomorphology, archaic crafts, obsolete musical instruments, classical architectural elements. The naming methodology is deliberate: the code-names operate as private analytical vocabulary that does not leak semantic meaning to casual external readers. Analysts who adopt this reference can speak of "the Pyrite-Anvil channel" or "the Estuary-Cobalt arrangement" with shared understanding while the language itself remains opaque.
