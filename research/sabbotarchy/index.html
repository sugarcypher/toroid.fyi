The Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy Signature

A measurement framework for engineered-incompetence regimes.

B. Greenway · ThinkWell Labs Metrology · May 2026

↗ Open full paper (Markdown, ~34kb)

Abstract

Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy — rule by sabotage (the sabbotarchic regime form) operating through deliberate obfuscation that frames the sabotage as incompetence (the obfuscratic operational signature) — names a regime character that has been observed informally but never measured formally. This paper proposes a five-dimensional signature framework that distinguishes engineered incompetence from genuine incompetence through measurable empirical patterns. A composite Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy Index emerges from the weighted aggregate of the five dimensions. The framework treats obfuscratic sabbotarchy as a measurement problem rather than a rhetorical accusation: the diagnosis must survive evidence, not produce it.

The bipartite naming

Both terms were coined by the author through linguistic-autopsy procedure — stripping phonemic material from base words to expose latent semantic content, then recombining with classical morphological suffixes to produce previously-unnamed analytical predicates.

Sabbotarchy names the regime form. The base word sabotage loses its initial phoneme and combines with the suffix -archy (Greek root meaning rule, as in monarchy, oligarchy, hierarchy). The choice of -archy over the colloquial -cracy is deliberate: -archy sits in the etymological family of regime forms (structural categories), while -cracy sits in the family of governance modalities (operational mechanisms). Sabbotarchy is a regime form, not a mode.

Obfuscratic names the operational signature. Obfuscation combines with the adjectival suffix -cratic, producing an adjective describing regimes operating through the deliberate production of confusion, framing-mismatch, and incoherent public information environments. Obfuscratic is a modifier, not a type.

Together: Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy names rule-by-sabotage that operates through deliberate obfuscation. A Sabbotarchy that operated transparently would be recognized and resisted. An Obfuscratic regime that didn't sabotage would have nothing to obfuscate. The compound captures the operational reality.

The five signature dimensions

Genuine incompetence and engineered incompetence are structurally distinct. The distinction is not magnitude — both can produce catastrophic outcomes — but pattern. The five dimensions operationalize the pattern-distinction.

S1 Outcome-Distribution Asymmetry weight 0.25

Divergence between the stated and realized beneficiary of an operation. Genuine policy produces approximately symmetric distributions — intent matches outcome. Engineered incompetence produces sharply asymmetric distributions: stated rationale operates as cover for actual objective. The OBBBA/Medicaid architecture demonstrates the framework cleanly — stated rationale of fiscal responsibility and work activation; realized outcome of ~$1.3T upward wealth transfer co-existing with 10.9–15M coverage losses producing documented downstream mortality.

S2 Domain-Selective Competence weight 0.20

Catastrophic failure-rate in public-facing functions co-existing with surgical precision in extractive functions — same architecture, same time-window, same personnel. The SSA pattern: catastrophic wait-time deterioration in benefit-delivery co-existing with surgical data-architecture mining powering cross-agency surveillance integration. The IRS pattern: termination of Direct File (working free filing tool) co-existing with retention of EITC audit capacity against low-income filers. The signature is differential capability within the same resource pool — only explicable by deliberate prioritization.

S3 Anti-Remediation Response weight 0.15

How resistance to correction varies as a function of correction cost. Genuine incompetence produces a positive cost-resistance slope (expensive fixes resisted more than cheap ones). Engineered incompetence produces a flat-high or inverted slope — cheap fixes resisted as strongly as expensive ones. The IG firings of January 2025 demonstrate this signature: reinstating the 17 fired IGs is effectively zero-cost (statutory requirement, IGs available, no new personnel required), yet resistance has been categorical. Maximum resistance, near-zero cost — unavailable to genuine incompetence.

S4 Recruitment Selection weight 0.10

Three-axis pattern: low technical-competence retention, high loyalty-signal advancement, near-zero dissent tolerance. The Schedule F architecture (January 2025) explicitly converts career civil service positions into political appointments, allowing technical-competence personnel to be replaced with loyalty-pipeline candidates without civil-service protections. The probationary employee terminations of February 2025 (~6,000 IRS, similar across HHS, DOJ, EPA) specifically targeted technical-competence early-career staff. The selection criterion is competence-presence rather than competence-absence.

S5 Information-Architecture Asymmetry weight 0.30 (highest)

The ratio between internal information coherence (within the regime) and external coherence (the public information ecosystem). Engineered incompetence produces extreme asymmetry: very high internal coherence flanked by very low external coherence. Four surrogates simultaneously deny that Project 2025 exists, defend its goals as obvious, dismiss its scope as exaggerated, and embrace its implementation as inevitable — and this multi-rationale incoherence is the operational point. External incoherence is the cover that allows internal coherence to proceed. Weighted highest because it is the dimension least available to alternative explanations.

The composite index

Index = 0.25·S1 + 0.20·S2 + 0.15·S3 + 0.10·S4 + 0.30·S5

BandRangeInterpretation
Genuine pattern0–30Consistent with genuine competence or incompetence
Mixed signature30–70Multiple dimensions present but not concentrated
Engineered pattern70–85Satisfies the engineered-incompetence signature
Extreme signature85–100Concentration is structural across all five dimensions

The thresholds are calibration conventions on the weighted mean, retained because the scored set's band membership survives ±0.05 weight perturbations. (An earlier claim that they were "derived from dimension count" — 70 ≈ four of five dimensions engineered, 85 ≈ all five — was withdrawn in paper v1.2: under unequal weights, four dimensions at 70 with the fifth at 30 produce composites of only 58–66.) Composite gaps within ~5 points are inside the scoring error band and are not ordinal claims — see the live instrument's robustness notes.

What the framework refuses

The framework treats obfuscratic sabbotarchy as a measurement problem rather than a rhetorical accusation. The diagnosis must survive evidence, not produce it.

An architecture scoring below 30 is consistent with genuine incompetence or genuine competence and the framework provides no basis for further diagnostic claim. The instrument is a measurement apparatus, not an accusation — and that distinction is what makes it useful.
