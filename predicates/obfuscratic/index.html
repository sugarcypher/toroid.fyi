Operational signature · coined modifier

Obfuscratic

A modifier — not a regime type. Names the operational signature of regimes that operate through the deliberate production of confusion, framing-mismatch, and incoherent public information environments.

Coinage · linguistic-autopsy procedure

obfuscation (noun, deliberate confusion-making)
+ adjectival suffix -cratic (operating via)
= Obfuscratic (adjective)

What it names

Obfuscratic is not a regime type. It is a modifier describing how a regime operates. The adjectival construction is essential — a noun would imply a structural category; the adjective correctly indicates an operational signature that can attach to many regime forms.

The signature appears as extreme asymmetry between internal coherence (within the regime, coalition, or operating architecture) and external coherence (the public information ecosystem the regime produces). The regime knows precisely what it is doing — coordination meetings, integrated implementation documents, aligned talking-point pipelines, sustained narrative architecture — while the public environment receives mutually-contradictory framings from coordinated sources.

The diagnostic instance

Four surrogates simultaneously deny that Project 2025 exists, defend its goals as obvious, dismiss its scope as exaggerated, and embrace its implementation as inevitable — and this multi-rationale incoherence is the operational point. The contradictions are not naturally arising; they are systematically produced. External incoherence is the cover that allows internal coherence to proceed.

Why the modifier matters

A Sabbotarchy that operated transparently would be recognized and resisted. An Obfuscratic regime that didn't sabotage would have nothing to obfuscate. Obfuscratic + Sabbotarchic = the operational reality; either alone would be analytically incomplete.

The modifier is the dimension weighted most heavily in the composite Obfuscratic Sabbotarchy Index (weight 0.30) because it is the most structurally specific. The other four signature dimensions have alternative explanations available (resource constraint, ideological commitment, normal political polarization); the information-architecture asymmetry signature has no alternative explanation that survives scrutiny. Obfuscratic operation is most legible here.

Measurement

The diagnostic gap is the quantitative measure: internal-coherence score minus external-coherence score, on 0–100 scales. Internal > 80 with external < 30 produces a gap > 50, which is structurally diagnostic of engineered information-environment.
