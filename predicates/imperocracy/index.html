Imperocracy

Rule by unbounded executive operating outside enforceable check. Names the structural condition — not a regime in itself, but the condition that lets a regime act without binding constraint.

Coinage · matrix-derived

imper- (Latin imperium: unbounded authority)
+ -ocracy (rule, governance mechanism)
= Imperocracy

What it names

Imperocracy is a property of the relationship between executive action and constraint structures, not of the executive actor itself. The mode operates when:

  • ·Adverse court rulings can be ignored or worked around without effective contempt enforcement
  • ·Statutory restrictions can be reinterpreted or selectively enforced without effective congressional response
  • ·Military or paramilitary force can be deployed for domestic operations without civilian oversight
  • ·Personnel decisions can be made without merit-system or civil-service protection
  • ·The executive's discretionary space expands faster than constraint structures can recover

The mode is distinct from authoritarianism per se. Authoritarianism is one possible operating regime under Imperocracy conditions, but Imperocracy describes the structural enabling condition for such regimes, not the regime itself. A democratic executive operating with normally-constituted constraint structures is not Imperocratic; the same executive operating after constraint structures have been weakened becomes Imperocratic regardless of stated democratic commitments.

How the mode was identified

Imperocracy emerged from sub-topology assembly in the Convergence Topology. Three cells — 7.13 (OBBBA contempt-enforcement limitation), 7.14 (mass immigration enforcement with due-process suspension), 7.15 (National Guard domestic deployment over state objection) — documented discrete policy actions. Reading them together revealed an operating system attacking the three load-bearing structures of constitutional order simultaneously. Generating the name made the configuration legible.

Distinction from adjacent predicates

PredicateHow Imperocracy differs
PathocracyPathocracy is a property of actors (psychological dysfunction). Imperocracy is a property of structural conditions; can operate under non-pathological executives.
CryptocracyCryptocracy is a property of opacity. Imperocracy can operate transparently — the mode is about unconstrained action, not hidden action.
AdversarocracyAdversarocracy is orientation toward conflict. Imperocracy can operate in non-adversarial directions; the mode is about constraint absence, not conflict preference.

Falsifier

Imperocracy is falsifiable. The mode is falsified by demonstration of effective binding check on executive action — sustained judicial enforcement of adverse rulings, congressional reassertion of authority, civil-military boundary restoration, civil-service protection enforcement. If conditions reversed, the mode would be falsified for the relevant period.

Anti-overuse rule

Imperocracy should not be tagged on cells merely because executive action is involved. The mode requires evidence that constraint structures are weakened or absent in the specific operational context.
